# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Check for updates automatically or from the Walldance menu, then install without downloading another installer.
- Choose automatic update checks and installation in Settings.

## 0.1.0

- Prepare dropped videos automatically and click to use them as wallpapers.
- Share one prepared video between the shelf and macOS, keeping originals untouched.
- Preview videos on hover with silent looping and automatic pausing.
- Remove videos from your shelf while keeping the originals.
